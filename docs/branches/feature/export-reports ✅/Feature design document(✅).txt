Branch:
feature/export-reports

Status:
Design Phase

====================================================
BUSINESS REQUIREMENT
====================

Customers should be able to export their order history.

The export should initially support:

1. CSV Export
2. Local Download

Future enhancements:

1. PDF Export
2. Email Export
3. Seller Sales Export
4. Generator-Based Streaming Export
